# Brew and Bean — Design System

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A coffee company presented as an extraction laboratory, refusing the cosy-cafe template.  
**OWN-WORLD:** Chalky paper `#F2F0E4`, signal orange `#F24405`, ink black, oversized PP Mori, crisp rules and trapezoidal photographic plates.  
**STORY:** Visitors move from the sensory craft of a cup to a precise partner for their workplace.  
**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed espresso machine photograph; almost-invisible top rail, restrained central two-line claim, quiet scroll prompt at lower left.  
**FORM:** Long-form gallery / laboratory log; three connected specimen plates rise around a centred scientific note, lock together into a single strip, then release upward. The enterprise chapter fades from paper to ink in-place before its details continue.

## Tokens

- Paper: `#F2F0E4`
- Primary signal: `#F24405`
- Secondary signal: `#F25C05`
- Ink: `#10100E`
- Typeface: `"PP Mori", "PPMori", Arial, sans-serif`

## Motion

The pointer is a delayed difference-blended disc. Links turn the disc orange and compress on press; drink rows expand it into a rectangular image preview. Scroll movement uses large, continuous visual changes—pinned specimen plates, spacious parallax stills, and an in-place light-to-dark transition—while respecting reduced-motion preferences.
