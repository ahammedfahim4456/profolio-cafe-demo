# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Coffee-led teams and hospitality operators choosing a specialty coffee partner for a cafe visit or workplace program.

## Product Purpose

Brew and Bean presents coffee as a controlled extraction practice: considered drinks for guests and a managed service for workplaces.

## Positioning

The brand treats every cup as a calibrated system rather than an incidental refreshment.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse curated drinks, visual work, and enterprise delivery details before opening a conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Single scrolling marketing experience. Commercial metrics and offerings below are illustrative brand content supplied in the brief.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Brew and Bean. Minimal brutalist character; PP Mori typography; paper ground with signal-orange accents; supplied extraction-lab imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

Hero reference and extraction-lab photograph at `public/images/extraction-lab.png`.

## Product Principles

- Make precision feel physical and desirable.
- Treat the page as an experience, not a catalogue.
- Keep every interaction clear enough to use at speed.
